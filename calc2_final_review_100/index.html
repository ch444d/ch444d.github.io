100 practice problems modeled after your Exam 1, Exam 2, and Exam 3 problem styles. Each problem includes a hidden answer.

Exam 1 style Area Between Curves

Problem 1

Find the area between \(f(x)=3x^2\) and \(g(x)=x^2+8\).
Answer
Intersections: \(3x^2=x^2+8\Rightarrow 2x^2=8\Rightarrow x=\pm2\). Area: \(\int_{-2}^{2}[(x^2+8)-3x^2]\,dx=\int_{-2}^{2}(8-2x^2)\,dx=\frac{64}{3}\).
Exam 1 style Area Between Curves

Problem 2

Find the area between \(y=x^2-4\) and \(y=2x\).
Answer
Intersections: \(x^2-4=2x\Rightarrow x=-2,4\). Area: \(\int_{-2}^{4}[2x-(x^2-4)]\,dx=36\).
Exam 1 style Area Between Curves

Problem 3

Find the area enclosed by \(y=9-x^2\) and \(y=x+3\).
Answer
Intersections: \(9-x^2=x+3\Rightarrow x=-3,2\). Area: \(\int_{-3}^{2}[(9-x^2)-(x+3)]\,dx=\frac{125}{6}\).
Exam 1 style Square Cross Sections

Problem 11

Problem 93

Determine whether \(\displaystyle \sum_{n=2}^{\infty}\frac{1}{n\ln n}\) converges or diverges.
Answer
Diverges by integral test: \(\int_2^\infty \frac{1}{x\ln x}dx=\ln(\ln x)\big|_2^\infty=\infty\).

Problem 95

Find the radius and interval of convergence for \(\displaystyle \sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{x^n}{n}\).
Answer
Radius \(R=1\). Endpoints: \(x=1\Rightarrow\sum1/n\) diverges; \(x=-1\Rightarrow\sum(-1)^n/n\) converges. Interval: \([-1,1)\).

Problem 98

Find the radius and interval of convergence for \(\displaystyle \sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{n(x-4)^n}{2^n}\).
Answer
Ratio/root test gives \(|x-4|/2<1\Rightarrow 2<x<6\). At endpoints terms do not go to \(0\). Interval: \((2,6)\), radius \(R=2\).

Problem 99

Determine whether \(\displaystyle \sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{n!}{10^n}\) converges or diverges.
Answer
Ratio test: \(L=\lim\frac{(n+1)!}{10^{n+1}}\frac{10^n}{n!}=\lim\frac{n+1}{10}=\infty\). Diverges.

Determine whether \(\displaystyle \sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{10^n}{n^n}\) converges or diverges.
Answer
Root test: \(L=\lim\sqrt[n]{10^n/n^n}=\lim\frac{10}{n}=0<1\). Converges.
